## Normal-service deployment finding

On 2026-09-13 the enabled local service unit executed
`/home/mgeronimo/.local/bin/mouse-control run`, importing
`/home/mgeronimo/.local/lib/python3.14/site-packages/mouse_control`.
That installed copy still selected `RatbagBackend`; it was not this native HID
checkout. Do not treat source-runtime testing as service validation until the
intended package is installed and the service unit is deliberately refreshed.
